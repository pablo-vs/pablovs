Pablo Villalobos Sánchez

About

I'm a generalist researcher interested in navigating the transition to a world with advanced AI. I'm currently a Research Scholar at GovAI, where I model loss of control risks. Previously, I worked on understanding data scaling and algorithmic progress at Epoch AI.

I also have a strong interest in the mathematics of intelligence and rationality, and pursue independent research on bounded rationality, decision theory, and compositional game theory.

My background is in mathematics and computer science from Universidad Complutense de Madrid, with experience spanning software engineering, data science, and AI research.
